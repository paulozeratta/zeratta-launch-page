Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

In short: we keep your first name, your email and your country, only to tell you about the launch. We do not sell, trade or hand these details to anyone. You can ask us to delete them at any time, without giving a reason, by writing to sac@zeratta.com.

Who handles your data

Zeratta is the controller of the data collected on this page. For anything to do with this policy, including deletion requests, the channel is sac@zeratta.com.

What we collect, and why

First name So we can address you by name in the launch messages.
Email To send the launch notice and the early access. It is the only channel we use.
Country To know where demand sits and plan availability, shipping and language.
Date and time you joined To order the queue by arrival, since going first is what we are promising.

We do not collect a phone number, a postal address, a document number, payment details, location data or anything sensitive. This page uses no cookies, no tracking pixel and no analytics tool.

On what legal basis

On your consent, given at the moment you submit the form. Zeratta is a Brazilian company, so the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018) applies, and where you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR applies as well. Under both, consent can be withdrawn at any time, and withdrawing it is as simple as asking us to delete your details.

Who else has access

Nobody buys, rents or receives this list. There are, however, infrastructure providers that process the data on our behalf, as processors:

  • Google: the spreadsheet and the script that receive the form run on Google Workspace, and the data is stored on Google infrastructure.
  • GitHub: this page is served by GitHub Pages, which records technical access data, such as your IP address, to run the service and prevent abuse.
  • Google Fonts: the body typeface is loaded from Google servers, which passes them your IP address at the moment the page loads.

How long we keep it

Until the launch and for the run of messages around it, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. If the campaign ends and you have not become a customer, the data is erased.

Your rights

Over your own data you have the right to:

  • confirm that processing exists and see what we hold;
  • correct anything incomplete, inaccurate or out of date;
  • ask for the deletion of data processed on your consent;
  • ask for it to be ported to another provider;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • know which parties we share it with.

To exercise any of these, write to sac@zeratta.com. We answer within 15 days. We do not ask for a reason and we will not try to talk you into staying.

Security

The page is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and the data travels encrypted to its destination. Access to the spreadsheet is restricted to Zeratta accounts. No system is impenetrable and we will not claim otherwise: should there be a security incident carrying material risk to you, we will tell you and notify the competent authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Changes that materially affect how your data is handled will be sent to you by email before they take effect.
